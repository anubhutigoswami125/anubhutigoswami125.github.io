01 / YOUR DATA

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

ALMONDS! is a browser-based educational guide hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs under its own policies.

Information you enter

The current version does not ask you to create an account, submit your name or email, or provide payment details. Search terms, filters and nutrition-calculator values are processed locally in your browser; the site code does not intentionally send them to a private database or server.

Cookies and third parties

The site does not intentionally set its own tracking cookies or include site-owned analytics. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and external links open third-party websites that operate under their own policies. Those services may process technical data independently.

Changes and contact

This educational site is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit sensitive information through linked services. The policy may change if features or data practices change. Contact the project owner through the repository with privacy questions.

Purpose and acceptable use

This site provides general educational information about edible nuts, seeds, food science and approximate nutrition. Do not misuse the site, attempt to disrupt it, or present its information as a professional diagnosis or certified laboratory analysis.

Nutrition figures are rounded estimates that vary by species, variety, brand, processing and preparation. Content is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat a condition. 04 / SHARED EXPECTATIONS

User agreement

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This agreement should be read alongside the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Using the guide

  • Use the encyclopedia and calculator for general learning and approximate comparisons.
  • Understand that values are estimates, not a guarantee for a specific packaged product or serving.
  • Check ingredient labels and allergen warnings; never use this site to decide whether food is safe for someone with an allergy.
  • Consult a qualified health professional for individual medical or dietary decisions.
  • Respect third-party websites, licences and applicable law when following links or reusing content.

Data and agreement

The current site has no sign-in, profile, comment system or submission form. Search, filters and serving-calculator interactions operate in the browser. If data-collecting features are added later, this agreement and privacy policy should be reviewed. Using the site indicates acceptance of this plain-language agreement; otherwise discontinue use.

05 / ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

Credits & sources

An independent educational web project. ALMONDS! combines pop-art styling, introductory botany and approximate nutrition information in a searchable field guide.

Research starting points

  • USDA FoodData Central — food composition and nutrient reference data.
  • U.S. Food and Drug Administration — food labelling and nutrition resources.
  • Food Allergy Research & Education — allergy education and safety.
  • Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew — plant science and botanical context.
  • NutritionValue — an additional food composition reference.

Design and technology

Original layout and interactions use HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Typography uses Archivo Black, DM Mono and DM Sans from Google Fonts. The pop-art almond illustration is created with CSS rather than a third-party stock image.

Accuracy note

Nutrition values are rounded educational estimates and may differ by cultivar, brand and preparation. Linked organisations are research starting points; this does not mean they reviewed or endorsed the site.

Curiosity, careful sourcing, and plant science.
