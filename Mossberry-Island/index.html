A note from the shore

The island didn't need a hero.
It needed someone to stay.

They say Mossberry appears only to those who need a place to begin again. Its cottages keep their keys. Its trees hold the memories of people long gone. And under the roots, something is waiting to wake.

You arrive with an empty satchel and no map. The rest is yours to discover — one small kindness, one curious detour, one story at a time.
