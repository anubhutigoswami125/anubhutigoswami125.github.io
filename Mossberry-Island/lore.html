The Mossberry Lorebook

Before the paths were paths and the cottages had doors, Mossberry was a seed carried over a very wide sea. These are the stories the island has kept safe.

🗝️

The Key That Waited

Long ago, the island kept a cottage for every person who needed somewhere to begin again. When the last boats left, the cottages were not locked. The island simply kept their keys safe.

Chapter I · Home
🌳

The Humming Trees

The first islanders planted trees close enough for their roots to meet. They learned each other's names beneath the soil and passed stories from leaf to leaf. That is why the grove still hums when the wind is gentle.

Chapter II · Memory
🌙

The Moon Beneath the Water

When Mossberry grew lonely, the moon stayed in the pond so the island would never have to look up at an empty sky. Even at noon, a little silver light remains beneath the ripples.

Chapter III · The promise
🪨

The First Dream

Before there were houses or paths, Mossberry was a seed carried across the sea. The old stones mark the place where it opened. The island was not discovered; it was dreamed into being by many small acts of care.

Chapter IV · Beginnings
🗺️

A Map of Elsewhere

The bottle's map shows no coastline. Instead it marks the places where someone was kind: a shared meal, a repaired roof, a light left on. The island grows wherever those things happen.

Chapter V · The wider world
🍓

The Berry That Glows

They say a Mossberry glows only when someone tells the truth nearby. No one knows who planted the first bush. But the berries grow brightest when a person finally admits what they came to the island hoping to find.

Why did the island go quiet?

The old stories disagree. Some say the islanders sailed away. Others say the island folded itself into a dream to protect something beneath its roots. The Moonpond remembers a different answer: “We did not leave. We went quiet.”
